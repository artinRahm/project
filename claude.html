1. What is the main character's name in Cyberpunk 2077?

The main character is V (short for Vincent/Valerie), a mercenary whose first name, voice, and background are chosen by the player during character creation.

2. What is the average percent of someone homeless in Cyberpunk 2077?

Night City doesn't have an official canonical homelessness statistic, but the setting portrays extreme wealth inequality — the vast majority of Night City's ~5+ million residents live in poverty or precarity in the sprawling megablocks and combat zones, while corporate elites live in luxury. Estimates from lore discussions suggest 60-80%+ of the population lives in conditions that would qualify as housing insecure or homeless by modern standards.

3. What is the setting of Cyberpunk 2077?

Night City, a fictional megacity in the Free State of Northern California, USA, in the year 2077. It's a dystopian cyberpunk metropolis dominated by megacorporations, rampant cybernetic augmentation, gang warfare, and extreme class stratification.

4. What game was Cyberpunk 2077 based on?

Cyberpunk 2077 is based on the tabletop RPG Cyberpunk 2020 (originally Cyberpunk 1988) by Mike Pondsmith and R. Talsorian Games. CD Projekt Red adapted the setting and mechanics, advancing the timeline from 2020 to 2077.

5. Who is the developer of Cyberpunk 2077?

CD Projekt Red (CDPR), a Polish video game developer and publisher known for The Witcher series.

6. What is the release date of Cyberpunk 2077?

December 10, 2020 (PC, PS4, Xbox One, Stadia). PS5 and Xbox Series X/S versions released February 15, 2022.

7. What happens to Jackie Welles in Cyberpunk 2077?

Jackie dies during the heist of the Relic (the biochip containing Johnny Silverhand's engram) at the Konpeki Plaza hotel. He is fatally wounded by Yorinobu Arasaka's security and passes the Relic to V before dying. His death is a pivotal moment that sets the main plot in motion.

8. What are the key features of the game?

Open-world RPG with first-person perspective; deep character customization (lifepaths, attributes, perks, cyberware); branching narrative with multiple endings; real-time combat with guns, melee, hacking (quickhacks), and stealth; vehicle driving; "Braindance" investigation mechanic; Night City open world with 6 districts; relationship/romance options; and the Johnny Silverhand (Keanu Reeves) co-protagonist storyline.

9. What are the main factions in Cyberpunk 2077?

Major factions include: Arasaka Corporation (Japanese megacorp, primary antagonist), Militech (US weapons/defense megacorp), Kang Tao (Chinese arms manufacturer), NetWatch (netrunner regulatory body), Voodoo Boys (Haitian netrunner gang), Maelstrom (cyberpsycho-prone gang), Valentinos (Latino gang in Heywood), 6th Street (ex-military/veteran gang), Animals (strength-augmented gang), Tyger Claws (Japanese gang in Westbrook/Watson), Scavengers (Scavs) (organ-harvesting gang), Aldecaldos (Nomad clan), and various smaller gangs and corporations.

10. What are the main story arcs in Cyberpunk 2077?

The main story follows V's struggle after the Relic biochip begins overwriting their mind with Johnny Silverhand's engram. Key arcs: Prologue/Lifepath (Street Kid/Nomad/Corpo origin) → The Heist (Konpeki Plaza, Jackie's death) → Act 1 (finding a fix, meeting Hanako Arasaka, Panam/Judy/River/Kerry questlines) → Point of No Return → Endgame paths: (1) Ask Hanako/Arasaka for help, (2) Storm Arasaka Tower with Rogue, (3) Storm with Panam/Aldecaldos, (4) Suicide (refuse all), (5) Phantom Liberty expansion path (Songbird/Reed). Each leads to distinct endings determining V's fate.

15. Who is Adam Smasher?

Adam Smasher (full designation: "Adam Smasher, Model: Smasher, Serial: 2023-001") is Arasaka's most infamous full-body conversion cyborg — a "corporate solo" and enforcer. Over 96% of his body is mechanical; he's essentially a walking tank. He led the Arasaka assault on the Night City Corporate Zone during the 4th Corporate War (2023), personally killing many, including (in lore) Johnny Silverhand's original body. In 2077, he serves as Yorinobu Arasaka's bodyguard and right hand. He's the final boss in most ending paths (Arasaka Tower assault). Nicknamed "The Night City Boogeyman." Formerly a US Army soldier named Adam, rebuilt by Arasaka after a near-fatal artillery strike.

16. Who is the Arasaka agent who helps you out?

Goro Takemura — former Arasaka executive bodyguard (Saburou Arasaka's personal protector) who goes rogue after Yorinobu frames him for his father's murder. He becomes a key ally in Act 1, helping V infiltrate Arasaka Tower and exposing Yorinobu's coup. His questline (Gimme Danger → Down on the Street → Who Wants to Live Forever) leads to the "Devil" ending where V accepts Hanako Arasaka's offer. He's honorable, loyal to the old Arasaka (Saburou), and one of the few genuinely "good" Arasaka operatives. Voiced by Masa Kanome.

19. Who is the street samurai who helps you out?

Jackie Welles is V's closest partner/best friend (street samurai/merc) in the prologue and Act 1 — he dies during the heist. Takemura (ex-Arasaka bodyguard) functions as a street samurai ally. Rogue Amendiares is a legendary former solo (street samurai) who helps in the "Sun" ending path. Adam Smasher is the antagonist street samurai (full-borg solo). In Phantom Liberty, Reed (NUSA/FIA operative) fills the elite solo role. There isn't one single "street samurai companion" throughout — V largely operates solo or with temporary allies depending on the path.

20. What is the H bomb in Cyberpunk 2077?

The "H-bomb" (or "hydrogen bomb") refers to the nuclear weapon detonated in Night City during the 4th Corporate War (2023). In the lore, Arasaka Corporation detonated a tactical nuclear device (often called the "Arasaka nuke" or "H-bomb") in the Corporate Center district to stop Militech and the Nomad assault on Arasaka Towers. This event — the Night City Holocaust — killed hundreds of thousands, leveled the city center, and created the "Hot Zone" (irradiated ruin) that still exists in 2077. It's referenced in shards, Johnny's memories, and the Arasaka Tower ground zero area. In gameplay, it's not an item you use — it's a historical event that shaped the city's geography and politics.

21. What's the deal with Johnny saying "time to party like it's 2023"?

This is one of Johnny Silverhand's most iconic lines, referencing the Night City Holocaust of 2023 — the climax of the 4th Corporate War when a tactical nuclear weapon (the "H-bomb") was detonated in the Corporate Center by Arasaka. For Johnny, 2023 was the year he led the assault on Arasaka Tower with his band Samurai and the Nomads, the year he lost his arm (replaced with a cybernetic one), the year his girlfriend Alt Cunningham was soulkilled, and the year he "died" (his body was killed by Adam Smasher, his engram captured by Arasaka). When Johnny says "time to party like it's 2023," he's being darkly ironic — he's referencing the chaotic, revolutionary, "burn it all down" energy of that night. It's a callback to his glory days as a rockerboy terrorist fighting the corps, before 50 years of being a digital ghost in the Relic. The line appears in key moments like the Chippin' In quest when he and V take back his Porsche, and during the Arasaka Tower assault in the "Sun" and "Temperance" endings. It encapsulates Johnny's philosophy: live fast, fight the system, and go out in a blaze of glory.
