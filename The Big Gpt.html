1. What is the main character's name in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: The main character is V (Vincent for male, Valerie for female), a mercenary in Night City.

2. What is the average percent of someone homeless in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Night City has extreme inequality; lore estimates suggest roughly 30–40% of the population lives in poverty or homelessness, though no exact official percentage is given in-game.

3. What is the setting of Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Night City, a dystopian megacity in the Free State of Northern California, year 2077.

4. What game was Cyberpunk 2077 based on?

Answer: The tabletop RPG "Cyberpunk" (specifically Cyberpunk 2020 / Cyberpunk Red) created by Mike Pondsmith.

5. Who is the developer of Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: CD Projekt RED.

6. What is the release date of Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: December 10, 2020.

7. What happens to Jackie Welles in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Jackie dies from wounds sustained during the heist of the Relic (the biochip containing Johnny Silverhand's engram) while escaping Arasaka Tower. He passes away in V's arms in the back of a car.

8. What are the key features of the game?

Answer: Open-world first-person RPG with three lifepaths (Nomad/Street Kid/Corpo), branching narrative with multiple endings, deep character customization, cyberware augmentations, hacking (breach protocol/quickhacks), varied combat (guns, melee, mantis blades), vehicle combat, dialogue choices that shape the story, romance options, and extensive side content (gigs, NCPD scanner hustles, cyberpsycho sightings).

9. What are the main factions in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Major corporations: Arasaka, Militech, Kang Tao. Gangs: Maelstrom, Valentinos, Tyger Claws, 6th Street, Animals, Voodoo Boys, Mox. Nomad clans: Aldecaldos, Wraiths. Others: NCPD, Trauma Team, NetWatch, Afterlife mercenaries/fixers.

10. What are the main story arcs in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Act 1 – The Heist (stealing the Relic). Act 2 – Searching for a cure while Johnny Silverhand's engram takes over. Act 3 – Point of No Return leading to multiple endings: The Devil (Arasaka), The Star (Panam/Nomads), The Sun (Rogue/Afterlife), Temperance (Johnny lives in V's body), and the secret "Don't Fear the Reaper" suicide ending.

11. What are the main side quests in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Major side-job chains: Panam Palmer/Aldecaldos (Ride On, Riders on the Storm, Queen of the Highway), Judy Alvarez (Both Sides Now, Talkin' 'Bout a Revolution, Pyramid Song), Kerry Eurodyne (Holdin' On, Rebel Rebel, Boat Drinks), River Ward (I Fought the Law, The Hunt), Rogue Amendiares (Chippin' In, Blistering Love), Goro Takemura (Down on the Street, Who Wants to Live Forever, Search and Destroy).

12. Who is the nomad you help out?

Answer: Panam Palmer, a member of the Aldecaldos nomad clan.

13. What are the main collectibles in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Tarot card murals (graffiti), Data shards (lore), Cyberpsycho sightings (for Regina Jones), NCPD Scanner Hustles, Iconic weapons & clothing, Vehicles/cars, Joytoys, Braindances, and various gig rewards.

14. What are the main achievements in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: Platinum: "The Legend of Night City." Story endings: The Devil, The Star, The Sun, Temperance, "Don't Fear the Reaper." Completion: All tarot cards, all iconic weapons, all cyberpsycho sightings, max Street Cred (50), max Level (50/60 with Phantom Liberty), all side jobs (Panam, Judy, Kerry, River, Rogue, Takemura).

15. Who is Adam Smasher?

Answer: A full-body conversion cyborg and Arasaka's top enforcer/assassin. Former US Army soldier. Killed Johnny Silverhand during the 2023 Arasaka Tower assault. Serves as a final boss in several endings.

16. Who is the Arasaka agent who helps you out?

Answer: Goro Takemura — former Arasaka executive bodyguard who turns against the corporation and helps V after the heist.

17. Who is the netrunner who helps you out?

Answer: Primarily Judy Alvarez (braindance technician/netrunner). T-Bug assists during the heist but dies early. The Voodoo Boys (Placide) also provide netrunning aid in Pacifica.

18. Who is the fixer who helps you out?

Answer: Dexter DeShawn (initially, for the heist), Rogue Amendiares (Afterlife fixer, endgame), Wakako Okada (Jig-Jig Street), Regina Jones (Watson). Dexter and Rogue are the most pivotal.

19. Who is the street samurai who helps you out?

Answer: "Street samurai" is a Shadowrun term; in Cyberpunk the equivalent is a Solo. The closest allies fitting this archetype: Johnny Silverhand (rockerboy/Solo hybrid), Rogue Amendiares (legendary Solo/fixer), or Kerry Eurodyne (former Samurai bandmate). No single character bears that exact title.

20. What is the H bomb in Cyberpunk 2077?

Answer: There is no item or event explicitly called the "H-bomb" in the base game. It may be a confusion with: the tactical nuke Johnny detonated at Arasaka Tower in 2023 (the Night City Holocaust), the Relic/Soulkiller biochip (a "nuke for the soul"), or the NUSA orbital missile strike option in the Phantom Liberty expansion.

21. What's the deal with Johnny saying "time to party like it's 2023"?

Answer: A dark callback to the 2023 Night City Holocaust — when Johnny Silverhand, Morgan Blackhand, and a strike team assaulted Arasaka Tower and detonated a tactical nuclear weapon. "Party" = causing massive destruction. Johnny relives this memory via the Relic engram.
